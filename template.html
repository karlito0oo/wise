About WISE Inc.

Transforming Waste into Sustainable Resources

WISE is a Filipino circularity company advancing practical, community-driven circular economy solutions across LGUs, schools, businesses, and underserved communities.

Our Mission

To build localized recovery systems and deploy appropriate technologies that convert waste streams into circular products, generating resilient livelihoods and empowering local waste workers and women.

Our Vision

Empowered Filipino communities building self-sustaining circular systems that significantly reduce waste, create inclusive opportunities, strengthen local economies, and support EPR and ESG commitments.
