# How to Update the WISE Website

This guide shows you how to change the words and pictures on the WISE website.
You don't need to know anything about computers or code. If you can send an email, you can do this.

---

## The big idea

Think of the website like a **notice board** in a hallway.

- Everyone can **look** at the notice board. That's the website: **wiseincph.com**
- Only a few people have the **key** to change what's on it. That's you!
- The place where you make changes is called the **Admin page**: **wiseincph.com/admin**

When you change something, nobody sees it until you press **Publish Changes**.
It's like writing a new notice at your desk. People only see it after you pin it on the board.

---

## Step 1: Open the Admin page

1. Open your internet browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox).
2. In the address bar at the top, type: **wiseincph.com/admin**
3. Press **Enter**.

You will see a box asking for your **Username** and **Password**.

## Step 2: Sign in

1. Type your **Username**.
2. Type your **Password**.
3. Click **Sign In**.

> 🔑 Your password is like a house key. Don't share it, and don't write it on a sticky note on your screen.
> If you forget it, ask another administrator to give you a new one (see "Managing administrators" below).

---

## Step 3: Pick what you want to change

On the side of the screen there is a **list of sections**. Each one is a part of the website.

| Section | What it changes on the website |
|---|---|
| **Site Identity** | The WISE name and logo |
| **Navigation** | The menu links at the top of the page |
| **Hero** | The big welcome message at the very top of the home page |
| **About** | The "who we are" part |
| **Ecosystem** | The part that explains how everything connects |
| **Services** | The list of things WISE offers |
| **Impact Calculator** | The little calculator visitors can play with |
| **Leadership** | The team members, with their names, jobs, and photos |
| **Implementation** | The steps of how WISE does its work |
| **Contact & Footer** | Phone, email, address, and the bottom of the page |
| **Recent Events** | News and photos from events |
| **Contact Form** | The words on the "send us a message" form |
| **Administrators** | Who is allowed to sign in here |

Click a section's name to open it.

---

## Step 4: Change the words

1. Find the box with the words you want to change.
2. Click inside the box.
3. Delete the old words and type the new ones, just like in an email.

At the top you'll see a little message:
- **"Unpublished changes"** means you changed something, but the website doesn't show it yet.
- **"All changes saved"** means the website matches what you see.

## Step 5: Publish (make it live!)

When you're happy with your changes:

1. Click **Publish Changes**.
2. Wait a second until you see **"Changes published."**
3. Click **View Website** to see your changes on the real website. 🎉

**Changed your mind?** Click **Discard Changes** before publishing. Everything goes back to how it was.

---

## Adding pictures

### Changing the logo
1. Go to **Site Identity**.
2. Click **Upload Logo**.
3. Pick the picture from your computer.
4. Click **Publish Changes**.

### Adding a photo of a team member
1. Go to **Leadership**.
2. Find the person.
3. Click **Upload Photo** and pick their picture.
   - If they already have a photo, the button says **Replace Photo**.
   - To take a photo away, click **Remove Photo**. The website will show the first letters of their name instead (like "JD" for Juan Dela Cruz).
4. Click **Publish Changes**.

### Adding event photos
1. Go to **Recent Events**.
2. Find the event.
3. Click **Upload Images** and pick one or more pictures.
4. Click **Publish Changes**.

### Picture rules 📷
- Use normal photo files: **JPEG, PNG, or WebP**. Photos from your phone are usually fine.
- Each picture must be smaller than **5 MB**.
- You can pick up to **8 pictures at a time**.

### What is "Image Description"?
Under each picture there is a box called **Image Description**.
Write one short sentence about what's in the picture, like *"Volunteers planting trees at the river."*
This helps people who can't see well, because their computer reads the sentence out loud to them.

---

## Adding, moving, and removing things in a list

Some sections are **lists**, like team members, services, or events.

- **Add something new:** click the button with the **➕ plus sign** at the top of the list, then fill in the boxes.
- **Move something up or down:** click the **⬆ up arrow** or **⬇ down arrow** next to it. The top of the list shows first on the website.
- **Remove something:** click the **🗑 trash can**. The website will ask if you're sure.

Don't forget to click **Publish Changes** when you're done!

---

## Managing administrators

An **administrator** is anyone who can sign in to the Admin page.

Go to **Administrators** in the side list.

- **Add a new person:** click **Add Administrator**. Type a username and a password that is **at least 12 characters long**, then type the password again to check it.
- **Change someone's name or password:** click **Edit**, make the change, and click **Save**. To keep their old password, leave the password box empty.
- **Remove a person:** click **Delete**. They will be signed out right away.

You'll see **(you)** next to your own name. To keep everyone safe:
- You **can't delete yourself**.
- You **can't delete the last administrator**. Somebody always needs a key!

---

## When you're done

Click **Sign Out**. It's like locking the door when you leave. This is extra important on a shared computer.

---

## Uh-oh! What does this message mean?

| Message | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **"Your session expired. Sign in again."** | You were away for a long time, so the page logged you out to stay safe. | Sign in again. |
| **"Another editor published changes…"** | Someone else changed the website while you were working. | Copy your new words somewhere safe (like Notepad), click **Reload Content**, then paste your words back and publish. |
| **"Unable to connect. Try again."** | The internet hiccupped. | Check your internet and try again in a minute. |
| **"Select up to 8 supported images, at most 5 MB each."** | A picture was too big or the wrong kind. | Pick a smaller photo, or pick fewer pictures at once. |
| **"Too many requests. Try again shortly."** | You clicked very fast many times. | Wait one minute, then try again. |

**Still stuck?** Contact the person who manages the website for WISE. Tell them what you clicked and what message you saw. A screenshot helps a lot!
